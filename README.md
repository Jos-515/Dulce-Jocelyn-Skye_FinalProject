# Dulce-Jocelyn-Skye_FinalProject

## What is the creative motivation / intended use of your project.
The creative motivation behind our Digital Aquarium was to explore how natural-looking ecosystems can emerge from simple programmed behaviours. By combining generative art and autonomous agents, the project aimed to recreate the movement, patterns and visuals of an underwater aquarium-type environment through code. That included adding behaviours such as flocking, seeking food and responding to the currents in the background to allow the environment to develop organically, producing unpredictable patterns and interactions. 

We also used the project as an opportunity to experiment with many of the different coding concepts learnt in class and display them in a clear demonstrative way, including autonomous agents, Perlin noise & sine waves. 

The intended audience is anyone interested in interactive digital art, generative systems or the natural world. We envisioned the aquarium as a calming, digital experience that invites viewers to observe the small interactions taking place within it. Much like watching a real aquarium, the audience should be able to appreciate the movement, visual harmony of the ecosystem without necessarily needing to interact with it directly.


## Describe how you implemented the project.
We decided to use different techniques that we learn during the course and use them for a specific element in our project. 
For the algae and crabs decoration we implemented W4-randomness and noise, for the water background we used W5- Randomness and noise, lastly for the fish we decided to use W6- Autonomous agents.
